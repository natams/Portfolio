Spin Fiesta

A free to play roulette game that shows how I think about onboarding, daily return and monetisation.

  • A welcome popup gives new players 5 coins, and returning players claim 2 coins every day.
  • Bet on red, black or any number from 0 to 36, with win streaks, bonus coins and a celebration that grows with the win.
  • When coins run out, a popup offers tomorrow's coins or a demo coin shop. Coins can also be redeemed for prizes.
  • Tracks key events and runs an A/B headline test through a URL parameter.

Promotional landing pages

NexPlay

Two interactive promotion pages for a casino and sportsbook brand, built to turn a standard offer into something players want to play.

  • Casino: a daily reward wheel. Players spin, see their reward and claim it, with separate flows for logged in and logged out players.
  • Sports: a football twist on Bet 10, Get 10. Players pick a spot in the goal and shoot for the chance to unlock 20 or 30 instead of 10.
  • All campaign content, from headlines and rewards to offer terms, lives in a config file, so it can change without touching the code.
  • Headline A/B tests run through a URL parameter, and both pages are mobile first and accessible.
